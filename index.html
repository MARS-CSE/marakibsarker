04 selected works
01
Undergraduate thesisFirst author

Explainable Zero-to-Few-Shot Learning for Brain Tumor Classification from MRI Images Using Semantic and Structural Feature Fusion

  • I developed and evaluated a brain MRI classification framework combining biomedical vision-language features, structural information and explainability-oriented analysis.
  • I focused on limited-label learning, patient-disjoint evaluation and interpretability.
Journal manuscript under review
02
ICCIT 2026First author

Quality-Supervised Reliability-Aware Text–Speech Fusion for Robust Bangla Sentiment Analysis

  • I developed a multimodal Bangla sentiment-analysis framework using BanglaBERT and XLS-R with reliability-aware fusion.
  • I evaluated clean, noisy and missing-modality conditions using leakage-controlled data.
Accepted for presentation
03
STI 2026Second author

Robustness and Tokenization Stability of Transformer-Based Bangla Dialect Classification Under Character-Level Perturbations

  • We benchmarked BanglaBERT, multilingual DistilBERT and XLM-RoBERTa across 59,453 dialect samples under progressively perturbed inputs.
  • We examined tokenizer stability, dialect-level performance and errors under character-level noise.
Accepted for presentation
04
Research manuscriptHealthcare analytics

Pseudo-Dynamic Mortality Prediction for Sepsis in LMIC Settings

  • We developed early-, mid-, and full-stage prediction models using 91,713 ICU admissions and compared LightGBM, Logistic Regression and Random Forest.
  • We used Optuna for optimization and SHAP for interpretability; our reported cross-validation AUC was 0.8913.
Manuscript under review
